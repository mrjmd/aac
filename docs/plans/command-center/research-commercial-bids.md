## 2. MA public bidding rules that gate eligibility

Building work under c.149 §44A (horizontal under c.30 §39M has the same \$10k/\$50k breakpoints):

| Value | Method | Meaning for a small sub |
|---|---|---|
| < \$10k | "Sound business practices" (c.30B §2) | Direct asks / phone quotes. **The relationship tier** with facility managers, school business managers, housing authority maintenance directors |
| \$10k-\$50k | Written scope to ≥ 3 contractors; 2+ weeks on COMMBUYS, Central Register, agency site, physical posting; ad can be skipped with 2 statewide-contract vendor quotes | Lowest responsible price. **The sweet spot.** EOHLC housing authority Small Projects Guide (\$0-50k) follows this |
| \$50k-\$150k | Sealed public bid opening; **5% bid deposit** | Bonding required |
| > \$150k | Formal c.149; **DCAMM certification for general bidders**; filed sub-bids for listed trades > \$25k; 100% performance + payment bonds | You'd be a sub to a GC or a filed sub-bidder |

- **Payment bond** on any public construction contract > \$25k, at least 50% of contract ([c.149 §29](https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXXI/Chapter149/Section29)); applies even on OSD Trades jobs ([TRD04 guide](https://www.statewidecontractuserguide.mass.gov/CUG/Guide/TRD04)).
- **Filed sub-bid trap:** "Waterproofing, Damp-proofing and Caulking" is a DCAMM filed sub-bid and crossover category. On c.149 projects > \$150k that work likely goes as a filed sub-bid needing DCAMM sub-bidder certification (projects completed in last 3 years, min \$20k project value, 3 successful evaluations). Whether crack injection falls in that section depends on the spec.
- **Prevailing wage: every public construction job, no dollar threshold**, including < \$10k. Weekly certified payroll; up to \$10,000 per violation; rate sheets per project, valid 90 days. HUD-funded housing authority work may add Davis-Bacon (not researched).
- **Bonding a tiny firm:** SBA Surety Bond Guarantee covers 70-90% of surety loss on contracts up to \$9M; apply through a surety agent; fee quoted at 0.6% (possibly dated). CPA-reviewed financials typical.
- MA Supplier Diversity Office SBPP is non-construction only.

## 3. Crawl / walk / run for a 3-person shop

**Crawl (month 1, free, mostly registration):** COMMBUYS with broad UNSPSC codes (concrete repair, waterproofing, masonry, building maintenance) and alerts to a parseable inbox; Central Register subscription; free profiles on BuildingConnected, PlanHub, ConstructConnect Bid Center, Procore with a South Shore / Greater Boston radius; register on OpenGov/BidNet/Euna for ~20 target towns and districts (Quincy, Braintree, Weymouth, Milton, Hingham, Hull, Quincy and Braintree housing authorities, etc.); ask OSD about TRD02/TRD01 re-bids.

**Walk (months 2-6, the weekly agent):** inputs are COMMBUYS/portal alert emails, the parsed Central Register PDF, GC ITB emails (BuildingConnected, Procore, SmartBid, PlanHub), optional SAM.gov API. LLM classifies trade fit, distance from Quincy, chapter/tier, bond/prevailing wage, filed sub-bid/DCAMM gates, site-visit and due dates; ranks; weekly digest. **Automatable:** discovery, dedupe, eligibility gating, document checklist, draft RFIs, first-draft price from the pricing ruleset, follow-ups on bid results. **Human:** "intend to bid" clicks and downloads inside GC/paid platforms (ToS), mandatory pre-bid walks, signed forms (non-collusion, tax certifications), bonds, final number and submission, anything signed under penalty of perjury.

**Run (6-18 months):** statewide contract slot (TRD02/TRD01); SBA-backed bonding; 3 public evaluations toward DCAMM sub-bidder certification if filed sub-bids are worth it; official APIs only (Autodesk; ConstructConnect paid API).

**Economics:** published win rates ~15-25% overall, 10-20% public low-bid, ~1 in 7 for subs (vendor blogs, not rigorous). Plan 5-10 bids per win; favor repeat on-call/term contracts.

## 4. Risks

- **ToS:** ConstructConnect bans AI agents/browser automation (use alerts or paid API); Autodesk bars scraping outside the API; PlanHub bars copying. COMMBUYS/BidNet/Dodge automation terms not found; check before crawling. Safe to automate: Central Register, COMMBUYS public view, SAM API, and inbound emails.
- **Prevailing wage:** certified payroll on every public job; reprices labor on those jobs; real penalties.
- **Bonding/cash:** 5% deposits > \$50k, payment bonds > \$25k, 100% bonds > \$150k; surety needs CPA financials, conflicting with the 2026 books state.
- **Capacity:** one tech, no large resurfacing until tech #2; fixed windows (school summers) and on-call response obligations.
- **Fit:** big c.149 / waterproofing filed sub-bid packages need DCAMM. Realistic: < \$50k 39M and housing authority work, school summer repairs, municipal on-call, private GC subcontracts.
